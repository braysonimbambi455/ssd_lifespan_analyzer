📊 Understanding Results

  • TBW = Total Bytes Written (manufacturer endurance rating)
  • Remaining TBW = Rating - Writes so far
  • Lifespan = Remaining TBW ÷ (Daily writes × 365 ÷ 1024)
  • Note: 1 TB = 1024 GB
